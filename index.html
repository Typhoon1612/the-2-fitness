Training Priorities

Keep the routine sustainable. Good technique and consistency matter more than rushing to increase weights.

For the first two weeks, use 2 sets for weighted movements if needed. Rest 60–120 seconds between sets. If the treadmill leaves your legs tired, rest before squats and deadlifts.

Core training can support pelvic control, but no single exercise guarantees correction of anterior pelvic tilt. Stop if you feel sharp pain, dizziness or unusual symptoms.
